# FIGURE_NOTES.md — defense figures

All figures are generated by `make_defense_figures.py` from the **saved result CSVs only**. No model
was retrained, no metric recomputed for plotting, no value invented, no CI altered, no result omitted.
Exported as PNG (300 dpi), SVG and PDF, 13.33 × 7.5 in (16:9), white background, `transparent=False`.

**Global scope statement (applies to every figure).** Patient-level FARFUM-RoP benchmark:
N = 1,528 images, 68 patients, 3 folds (seed 42), zero patient overlap, 3 classes, identical OOF
evaluation population for every method. `Previous Lab B5` is a **3-class reimplementation** of the
previous laboratory architecture; `ROPDeepX` is a **harmonized manual implementation**. Neither is a
published headline result. No figure claims state of the art, best model, or clinical readiness.

**Metric direction (stated once, applies everywhere).** Macro OvR ROC-AUC, balanced accuracy and macro
F1 are **higher is better**; Brier and ECE are **lower is better**. Brier and ECE are never plotted on
the same axis as the ranking metrics.

**Value verification.** Every plotted number and every CI endpoint was compared with the saved CSV
before plotting (`_figs/verify_values.py`): 145 numeric comparisons + 22 integrity checks, all PASS for
the plotted values. Two **rounding-level discrepancies were found in the supplied ground-truth text
block** (both in values that are NOT plotted in any figure) and are reported instead of silently
resolved:

| supplied ground-truth value | saved result | note |
|---|---|---|
| per-fold fold-0 G AUC = 0.710 | 0.709476 → 3 dp = **0.709** | rounding of the supplied value goes the wrong way; no figure uses per-fold values |
| G−E p = 0.321 | 0.3205 | exact rounding boundary (0.3205 → 0.321 at 3 dp); Figure 3 prints the saved p = 0.321 |

## Figure 1 — `01_fair_benchmark_auc` (PNG / SVG / PDF)

1. **Source CSV:** `results/fast_benchmark/04_common_metrics.csv`
2. **Columns used:** `key` (method), `auc_macro_ovr`
3. **Every value plotted** (exact CSV values): `Legacy` 0.640405, `ROPDeepX` 0.624568, `B` 0.712618,
   `C` 0.712870, `E` 0.707739, `G` 0.709406. Printed on the bars as 0.6404 / 0.6246 / 0.7126 /
   0.7129 / 0.7077 / 0.7094. X-axis is **zoomed to 0.55–0.75 and labelled "Zoomed AUC axis (0.55 – 0.75)"**.
4. **What it DOES show:** the macro OvR AUC of six methods evaluated on exactly the same 1,528
   held-out images and the same patient-level folds; the two harmonized comparator reimplementations
   (0.6404, 0.6246) sit below the four current RGB-embedding models (0.7077–0.7129).
5. **What it does NOT show:** it does not show whether any difference is distinguishable from zero —
   that is Figure 2; it does not show calibration (Brier/ECE) or thresholded decision quality
   (balanced accuracy, macro F1 — see Figure 5); it does not show any published ROPDeepX or previous-lab
   number; it does not show clinical utility.
6. **20-second defense explanation:** "All six methods were scored on the same 1,528 FARFUM images,
   the same 68 patients and the same three patient-level folds, by one metric script. The two
   harmonized reimplementations score 0.6404 and 0.6246; the current RGB-embedding family scores
   0.7077 to 0.7129. The axis is zoomed to 0.55–0.75, and it is labelled as such; the paired
   confidence intervals are in the next figure."
7. **Likely examiner questions / safe answers**
   - *"Is the zoom hiding a small effect?"* → "The axis is explicitly labelled zoomed; the full
     comparison to chance and to the comparators is shown, and all differences are tested with paired
     patient-level bootstrap CIs in Figure 2."
   - *"So E is your best model?"* → "E is the predefined main vessel-fusion model; C has the highest
     point AUC here (0.7129), and no claim of overall model superiority is made."
   - *"Are these the published numbers?"* → "No. Legacy is a 3-class reimplementation and ROPDeepX is a
     harmonized manual implementation; published headline values are context only."
8. **Required statement (also printed on the figure footer):** *Current C has the highest point AUC in
   this single-source benchmark, but no claim of overall model superiority is made.*

## Figure 2 — `02_fair_benchmark_delta_auc_forest` (PNG / SVG / PDF)

1. **Source CSV:** `results/fast_benchmark/05_paired_statistics.csv`
2. **Columns used:** `comparison`, `metric` (= `auc`), `delta`, `ci_lo`, `ci_hi`
3. **Every value plotted:**
   - E − ROPDeepX: ΔAUC = +0.083171, 95% CI [+0.024002, +0.144865]
   - E − Previous Lab B5: ΔAUC = +0.067334, 95% CI [+0.003095, +0.133092]
   - E − Current B: ΔAUC = −0.004878, 95% CI [−0.011221, +0.001549]
   Dashed vertical line at ΔAUC = 0.
4. **What it DOES show:** paired, patient-level bootstrap differences (10,000 replicates, seed 42) with
   95% percentile CIs; the CIs for E vs the two harmonized comparators exclude zero, the CI for E vs
   Current B crosses zero.
5. **What it does NOT show:** no significance stars (none are supported by the saved analysis at a
   corrected level and none are claimed); no ranking of published methods; no information about
   Δmacro F1 or ΔBrier (those are in the same CSV: E−ROPDeepX ΔF1 +0.1271 [+0.0604, +0.1982],
   ΔBrier −0.0461 [−0.1794, +0.0825]; E−Legacy ΔF1 +0.1166 [+0.0416, +0.1991], ΔBrier +0.0837
   [−0.0504, +0.2108]; E−B ΔF1 −0.0002 [−0.0089, +0.0084], ΔBrier +0.0046 [−0.0110, +0.0202]).
6. **20-second defense explanation:** "Each row is a paired comparison on the identical patients, so
   the same patient contributes to both methods in every bootstrap replicate. E is higher than the
   harmonized ROPDeepX implementation by 0.083 AUC and than the 3-class legacy reimplementation by
   0.067, with CIs excluding zero; against Current B the difference is −0.005 with a CI that includes
   zero, so it is not distinguishable."
7. **Likely examiner questions / safe answers**
   - *"Why are the comparator differences so large?"* → "Both comparators were retrained inside every
     fold on 736–793 images with their own faithful recipes; the interval reflects that comparison,
     not the published models."
   - *"Is 0.083 clinically meaningful?"* → "No clinical claim follows. It is a discrimination
     difference on one source. ΔBrier for that comparison has a CI crossing zero."
   - *"Does a CI crossing zero prove equivalence?"* → "No. It means this sample does not distinguish the
     two; absence of evidence is not evidence of absence."
8. **Required statement:** *E has supported positive AUC differences versus the two harmonized
   comparator implementations, but not versus Current B.*

## Figure 3 — `03_patient_cv_sensitivity_forest` (PNG / SVG / PDF)

1. **Source CSV:** `results/fast_examiner/07_paired_bootstrap.csv`
2. **Columns used:** `comparison`, `metric` (= `multiclass_auc`), `delta`, `ci_lo`, `ci_hi`, `p_value`,
   `n_replicates`
3. **Every value plotted:**
   - C − B (add five biomarkers): ΔAUC = +0.000252, CI [−0.003536, +0.003907], p = 0.889
   - E − B (add vessel representation): ΔAUC = −0.004878, CI [−0.011221, +0.001549], p = 0.132
   - G − E (add biomarkers after vessel): ΔAUC = +0.001667, CI [−0.001663, +0.004975], p = 0.321
   All three from 10,000 patient-level bootstrap replicates, seed 42. Bottom annotations: decision
   values read from `results/fast_examiner/11_decisions.json`
   (`K_FOLD_BIOMARKER_CONCLUSION = ROBUST_NULL`, `K_FOLD_VESSEL_CONCLUSION = NULL`).
4. **What it DOES show:** the examiner-driven sensitivity analysis — the biomarker and vessel
   increments re-estimated under patient-level 3-fold cross-validation instead of one fixed split; all
   three CIs include zero in this single-source regime.
5. **What it does NOT show:** it does not show that the vessel representation "fails" or is useless;
   it does not replace the canonical locked multi-source test (where E−B was +0.0079
   [+0.0026, +0.0131]) or the cross-source held-out fold (+0.0222 [+0.0148, +0.0299]); it does not
   include the feature-selection variants (FS1/FS2 in `05_feature_sensitivity.csv`).
6. **20-second defense explanation:** "This figure answers the question directly: the whole comparison
   was repeated under patient-level 3-fold cross-validation, with both encoders retrained inside every
   fold. C−B, E−B and G−E all stay within ±0.005 with confidence intervals that include zero, so the
   weak biomarker result was not an artefact of one fixed split."
7. **Likely examiner questions / safe answers**
   - *"Why is the vessel effect gone here?"* → "This is a smaller, single-source, in-domain regime with
     771 images per fold; the canonical multi-source test and the cross-source held-out fold are
     different regimes and are reported separately."
   - *"Does this mean the five biomarkers are useless?"* → "No. It means they added no measurable
     increment beyond the RGB and vessel representations in this protocol, and the redundancy audit
     shows the panel is largely collinear."
8. **Required statement:** *The five-scalar biomarker null result survived patient-level
   cross-validation. The spatial vessel increment did not replicate in this single-source setting.*

## Figure 4 — `04_biomarker_redundancy_heatmap` (PNG / SVG / PDF)

1. **Source files:** Pearson matrix computed from `results/fast_examiner/02_common_farFUM_3fold_manifest.csv`
   (columns `vessel_density_fov`, `skel_density_fov`, `fractal_d0`, `fractal_d1`, `fractal_d2`;
   n = 1,528 rows); VIF annotation from `results/fast_examiner/04_biomarker_redundancy.csv`
   (`scope == farfum_all`, `vif`); PCA annotation from
   `results/fast_examiner/05c_fs_detail_fold{0,1,2}.json` (`FS2` = 1 in all three folds).
2. **Columns used:** the five biomarker columns (matrix); `vif` (annotation).
   **Note:** `04_biomarker_redundancy.csv` stores per-feature extremes and the fractal pairwise
   correlations, not the full 5 × 5 matrix. The matrix was therefore computed from the saved cohort
   and **cross-checked against the CSV** — r(fractal_d1, fractal_d2) = 0.995329, r(fractal_d0,
   fractal_d1) = 0.975392, r(fractal_d0, fractal_d2) = 0.956175, all matching the CSV values exactly.
   Nothing was fabricated.
3. **Every value plotted** (25 cells, 2 decimals): diagonal 1.00; vessel_density–skel_density 0.88;
   vessel_density–fractal_d0 0.74; vessel_density–fractal_d1 0.71; vessel_density–fractal_d2 0.70;
   skel_density–fractal_d0 0.51; skel_density–fractal_d1 0.47; skel_density–fractal_d2 0.44;
   fractal_d0–fractal_d1 0.98; fractal_d0–fractal_d2 0.96; fractal_d1–fractal_d2 1.00.
   The three collinear pairs are listed in the annotation block with 3 decimals
   (0.995 / 0.975 / 0.956) because 2-decimal display rounds 0.995 to 1.00.
   Annotations: **Max VIF ≈ 366** (366.087), **PCA ≥95 % variance: 1 component** (all three folds).
4. **What it DOES show:** the five scalar biomarkers are strongly collinear — the three fractal
   dimensions share most of their variance (r = 0.956–0.995) and the variance-inflation factors are
   large (up to ≈366); a PCA that keeps 95 % of the variance needs one component in every fold.
5. **What it does NOT show:** it does not show that the panel contains only one disease-relevant
   dimension, nor that any feature is predictive or non-predictive; VIF and PCA are properties of the
   feature *variance*, not of disease signal; it does not show a causal relation to the null result.
6. **20-second defense explanation:** "The five biomarkers are not five independent measurements. The
   three fractal dimensions correlate 0.96 to 0.995, the largest VIF is about 366, and a PCA keeping
   95 % of the variance needs a single component in all three folds. So the panel is largely one
   collinear axis of information — which is a property of the features, not proof that only one
   disease dimension exists."
7. **Likely examiner questions / safe answers**
   - *"So the panel is one dimension — is that why it failed?"* → "It is a plausible contributing
     explanation, consistent with the correlation-pruning and PCA sensitivity analyses leaving the
     C−B and G−E conclusions unchanged; we do not claim it as the sole cause."
   - *"Why is the matrix not directly in the CSV?"* → "The CSV records per-feature VIF/variance and the
     fractal pairwise correlations; the full matrix was computed from the saved cohort and matches the
     CSV values exactly."
8. **Required statement:** *High redundancy among fractal dimensions does not prove that the panel
   contains only one disease-relevant dimension.*
